# Track selection

From the supplied **Challenge Topics**, SP-B Knowledge Evolution Engine is the most technically complex: it combines graph/RAG knowledge building, three-tier memory, fine-tuning, retrieval, intent evolution, world-model prediction and constrained decisions. It has the largest data and evaluation burden.

SP-D Organizational Collaboration Office is the strongest fit for this entry's live demonstration. A judge can give one RFQ, see choices and staff routing, inspect task evidence and escalation, then receive a schedule and reports. That makes technical depth, business usefulness and user experience visible in one short story. It is not a guarantee of an award.

The supplied **Judging Rubric** requires six valid, independently testable skills and assesses technical quality (25), application value (25), verifiable expert knowledge (20), innovation and UX (15), and demo (15). The supplied **Submission Guideline** uses a different, broader Track B weighting, so confirm the organizer's final submission form and current rules before entry. The package's simulation evidence is honest but cannot replace real enterprise authorization, provider contracts or a live deployed workspace.
