# Source register and distillation

The reference enterprise is [四川上航假期国际旅行社有限公司](https://www.scsatrip.com/). Its public site describes travel agency services, destination operations, business travel and MICE, and publishes group-client contact details. Its September 2026 homepage notice discusses holiday travel safety management. Those observations motivate the RFQ, supplier, safety, finance and client-response stages. The company did not provide this software, roster, prices, SOPs, or approval rules.

[UNESCO's Chengdu City of Gastronomy profile](https://www.unesco.org/en/creative-cities/chengdu) supports the relevance of food and cultural experiences in a Chengdu itinerary. The packaged activity names and costs are invented examples; the source does not endorse those products or prices.

| Public observation | Packaged decision |
| --- | --- |
| Travel agency and destination services | Separate sales intake, itinerary design, supplier verification and operations duties |
| Business and group travel offering | Group size, seat capacity and budget constraints in flight and quote comparison |
| Public safety emphasis | A safety-clearance task blocks client release until evidenced |
| Chengdu gastronomic context | Food and tea interests influence tour search and scheduling |

All staff identities, reporting lines, capacity, dates, flight carriers, seats, costs, activity inventory and `SIM:` evidence in `resources/` and `output/` are synthetic. The CNY 100,000 executive gate, margin and task durations are configurable demonstration rules, not an enterprise policy or an asserted legal requirement. A production deployment would replace these with authenticated company SOPs, supplier contracts, actual booking APIs and a review of applicable travel rules.
