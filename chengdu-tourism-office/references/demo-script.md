# SP-D live demo and judging narrative

The final [agent-led launch recording and script](../../submission/demo-script.md) supersede this tourism-only rehearsal. The recorded run completed 20 skill executions across 16 distinct skills and 7 fictional colleagues, producing 10 real files. It used 24 model requests and 26 tool calls; reported usage totaled 800,795 tokens across requests. Actual wall time was 303.8 seconds. The scenario below remains an additional demonstration of the tourism engine.

## Claim

One ordinary sentence can trigger an auditable, cross-functional company workflow. The differentiator is not a single chatbot answer: Atlas Office represents an organization as programmable staff agents with capacity, skills, managers, follow links, role guidance and a task-specific collaboration network. It drives a request from intake to a gated management outcome and customer-ready artifacts.

## Five-minute run

Before presenting, select **Chengdu tourism** in Workspace settings, select **Proposal first**, and leave real email off. Use a new conversation. General office tools remain available in Skills library.

1. **0:00–0:40 — The ask.** Send the one-sentence RFQ in Assistant. Explain that missing fields become explicit simulation assumptions, not hidden inventions. Show the saved conversation and pending proposal.
2. **0:40–1:35 — The organization.** Open the relationship network; toggle reporting, collaboration and follow links. Click Sales Manager Wang and ask “What is the quote?” Then ask “Who is leading and what are my tasks?” Play one answer aloud. Edit a staff member’s capacity or decision style, rerun, and point out changed routing or option priority.
3. **1:35–2:25 — Decision quality.** Open Work & decisions → Options. Compare full-package alternatives, rejected constraints, flight group estimates and tour capacity. Make clear these are synthetic fixtures and not bookable fares.
4. **2:25–3:20 — Closed-loop execution.** Return the proposal with comments, revise the budget in chat, and show that the group, dates and review history are retained. Ask the manager about a blocker, resolve it with guidance, resubmit, then approve simulated execution. Point to dependencies, approval gates and reporting routes. Explain that task buttons cannot bypass the proposal gate.
5. **3:20–4:15 — Delivery.** Show the dated schedule, customer email draft, Word brief, PowerPoint deck and evidence register. Search for “tea” and reopen the saved RFQ. Email remains unsent; describe the separately gated MCP send tool without invoking it.
6. **4:15–5:00 — Technical proof.** Nine independently callable skills, 20 behavior checks per skill, shared JSON envelope, local FTS5 index, MCP tools and SMTP approval gate. Mention the enterprise facts/simulation boundary and the precise path to real provider adapters.

## Honest limits and next validation

- Flight and tour prices are synthetic, dated to the request only for the demo, and need a real provider plus group-seat confirmation. No booking is made.
- Staff portraits are fictional and use browser TTS with speaking-state highlights, not lip-synced video. OpenAI conversation is optional; no-key mode is explicitly local. Tourism execution is a deterministic simulation. Decision style affects option priority when selected as lead, while skills, availability and projected load affect routing.
- SMTP delivery is opt-in with a recipient allowlist, configured credentials and the chosen proposal policy. The separate MCP email tool requires an approval code. External writes from general office skills always need review in Activity. Leave delivery disabled during the demo unless explicitly testing a controlled mailbox.
- A manager escalation is recorded in the app with the blocker and guidance; it is not an email to a real manager.
- The company profile uses public-source context only. Fictional employees and reporting lines are not claims about the real organization.
- Before production use: obtain company permission, connect approved suppliers, implement authentication and audit access control, review personal-data handling, and run real operator tests.
