# Tourism RFQ decision rules

These rules belong to the demonstration model; they are not the named company's SOP.

- Quote options must fit customer budget, group size, preparation time and selected travel dates.
- A flight offer is a snapshot. Group seat count and final price require supplier confirmation.
- Activity matches must respect group capacity and accessibility needs.
- Quotations cannot be released until supplier, safety and finance evidence exists. Quotes at or above CNY 100,000 also require an executive approval task.
- Task assignments require a relevant skill, availability and at least two remaining hours of capacity. Cross-branch assignments route through the reporting hierarchy.
- The customer email remains a draft until a human approves sending; the MCP server additionally checks its approval code and recipient allowlist.
- Every synthetic price, person and evidence reference must remain visibly marked as a simulation.
